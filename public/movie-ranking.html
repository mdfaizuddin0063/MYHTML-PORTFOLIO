      2. The Godfather (1972) Director: Francis Ford Coppola Genre: Crime, Drama

      5-Point Search:

      Plot – Chronicles the powerful Italian-American mafia family of Don Vito Corleone and the transformation of his son, Michael.
      Legacy – Frequently ranked as one of the greatest films ever made; preserved in the U.S. National Film Registry.
      Awards – Won 3 Oscars, including Best Picture, Best Actor (Marlon Brando), and Best Adapted Screenplay.
      Performances – Marlon Brando and Al Pacino delivered iconic, career-defining roles.
      Cultural Impact – Influenced decades of gangster films and popularized mafia tropes in pop culture.

      3. The Shawshank Redemption (1994) Director: Frank Darabont Genre: Drama

      5-Point Search:

      Plot – Follows Andy Dufresne, a banker wrongfully imprisoned, and his friendship with Red, a longtime inmate. Source Material – Based on Stephen King's novella Rita Hayworth and Shawshank Redemption. Fan Favorite – Holds the #1 spot on IMDb's Top 250 Movies (as of 2025). Themes – Hope, resilience, and the human spirit in the face of despair. Cult Status – Initially underperformed at the box office but gained massive acclaim over time through word-of-mouth and home video.
